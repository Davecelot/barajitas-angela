- **1:** team badge / association crest, usually shiny
- **2-12:** player stickers

- **14-20:** player stickers

Special sections visible in the photos:

- **FWC:** FIFA World Cup history / host city / official-content stickers
- **CC:** Coca-Cola section

Status legend:

- **Placed:** a physical sticker is already pasted into the album slot.
- **Left:** the printed placeholder is visible and the sticker is not pasted.
- **Uncertain:** slot is blocked, cropped, glared out, or too blurred to classify safely.
